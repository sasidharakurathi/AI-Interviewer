
Problem Statement:
    To, develop an "AI interviewer" with two main user roles 1. Candidate and 2. Recruiter.

Core Features:
    Candidate Features:
        1. Interactive video interview interface with camera and microphone access.
        2. Real-time speech-to-text and text-to-speech conversion.
        3. Comprehensive interview feedback and history.
        4. Profile management.

    Recruiter Features:
        1. Custom interview creation based on the job descriptions.
        2. Live Interview monitoring.
        3. Detailed candidate anaylsis dashboard.
        4. Detailed reports with conversasion transcripts.
        5. Question customiaztion and interview management.


Technologies:
    1. "WebRTC" implementaion for video streaming.
    2. "Django Channels" for WebSocket signaling and real-time updates.
    3. "OpenCV + DeepFace" for computer vision. (to detect cheating etc.)
    4. "Ollama LLM" for question generations and response analysis. 
    5. "Whisper/SpeechRecognition" for speech processing.
    6. "Ffmpeg" for Audio or Video processing.

Open Source (Free) Technologies:
    1. LLM                      - Ollama's Llama 3.2 3B (Local)
    2. Speech-to-Text           - OpenAI Whisper (Local)
    3. Text-to-Speech           - pyttsx3 and gTTS.
    4. Computer Vision          - OpenCV + DeepFace (DeepFace with VGG-Face)
    5. Video Communication      - WebRTC + Django Channels
    6. Database                 - MySQL
